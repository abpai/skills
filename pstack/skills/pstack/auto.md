# Auto

Take the task through planning, implementation, and verification.

1. Use [plan.md](plan.md) to research the request, select relevant principles,
   and form a concise plan. Continue into implementation rather than stopping
   at the plan.
2. Implement the plan, applying the selected principles. Refine the approach
   when new evidence warrants it while preserving the requested outcome.
3. Use [verify.md](verify.md) to review the implementation and run its checks.
   Fix the findings, then repeat the affected checks until they pass or a
   concrete blocker prevents progress.

Return a short summary of what changed, the selected principles and what each
shaped, the checks run and their results, and any remaining gaps or blockers.
Do not claim completion beyond what was verified.
