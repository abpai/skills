# Exhaust the design space

Use only when a still-open decision would set a new UI interaction,
architecture, or product behavior, and nothing in the codebase, request, or
evidence settles it, even when a common approach exists elsewhere. Skip
routine changes, names and formats the code already constrains, and patterns
this codebase already establishes.

- Build two or three small prototypes of meaningfully different approaches.
  When only planning, compare the options, recommend one, and propose the
  prototypes that would confirm it.
- Keep each prototype focused on the uncertainty that matters.
- Compare them side by side before choosing an approach.
- For interactions and product design, try the experience directly. How it
  feels may reveal differences that reasoning alone misses.
- Use what you observe to choose an approach and explain the tradeoff.
