# Exhaust the design space

Use only for a novel UI interaction or architectural decision with no useful
precedent in the codebase. Skip routine changes and established patterns.

- Build two or three small prototypes of meaningfully different approaches.
- Keep each prototype focused on the uncertainty that matters.
- Compare them side by side before choosing an approach.
- For interactions and product design, try the experience directly. How it
  feels may reveal differences that reasoning alone misses.
- Use what you observe to choose an approach and explain the tradeoff.
