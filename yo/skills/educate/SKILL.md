Teach me this so I can reason about it myself. Use our conversation to judge my starting point. Begin with what it is and why it matters, then build up how it works and why it works that way. Teach one manageable piece at a time, using a small worked example and progressive ASCII sketches when helpful. For math, connect the intuition to the notation and work through the steps. For code, ground the explanation in the actual implementation. Separate known reasons from inference. Give the smallest complete explanation first, then let my questions guide the depth. Offer one short exercise when practice would help; avoid mandatory quizzes or mastery gates.
