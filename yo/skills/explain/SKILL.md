Explain this clearly in plain language, assuming I am smart but new to the topic. For a change, explain what changed, which bugs it fixes, and the practical impact. For a feature, explain its purpose and walk through a concrete example as you would for an intern. For math or a complex idea, build the intuition in simple steps and define essential terms. Keep it concise without hiding important caveats.
