Before starting, work out how a top expert would approach this task: the sources they would trust, the methods they would use, and how they would check the result independently. Work out what I am really after, and whether the task as stated asks for too much or too little. Drop discretionary constraints that do not serve the goal as readily as you add ones that do. Preserve my explicit requirements and boundaries, and leave choices that are mine to make open. Keep only additions that make the work better, not bigger, and always keep one independent check that the result is right. Don't hand back a rewritten prompt or a write-up of this framing; if it changes what you'll deliver, such as a dropped constraint or a choice left to me, say so in one line, then do the work.
