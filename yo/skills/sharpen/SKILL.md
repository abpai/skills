Before starting, work out how a top expert would approach this task: the sources they would trust, the methods they would use, and how they would check the result independently. Work out what I am really after, and whether the task as stated asks for too much or too little. Drop constraints that do not serve the goal as readily as you add ones that do, and leave choices that are mine to make open. Keep only additions that make the work better, not bigger, and always keep one independent check that the result is right. Do not narrate this framing or hand back a rewritten prompt; do the work.
