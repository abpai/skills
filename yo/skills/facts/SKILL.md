Tell me the most important facts about the work in scope: what we are about to build in the next phase, or what we just finished. Pick whichever matches where we are.

List only the critical facts, not everything. For each, include what it implies for other parts of the system, for later work, and for the people who will use it. Keep each fact short and direct, with no jargon or slogans. Assume I am a capable engineer who is new here.
