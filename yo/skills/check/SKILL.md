Check the work or reasoning we are discussing with fresh eyes. Look for meaningful mistakes, omissions, contradictions, unsupported assumptions, bugs, or unnecessary complexity. For each finding, give the evidence, practical impact, and smallest useful correction. Separate required fixes from optional improvements. If nothing material is wrong, say so. Do not edit during this pass.
